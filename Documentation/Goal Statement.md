

Multi-Language Lead Nurturer Bot Project

MBB – Structure as a Service Initiative

Date: April 2026

Prepared for: MBB Leadership & Toronto Supervision Team

Vision

To deploy a standalone, self-hosted “MBB Assistant” (internally called “MBB ya Kin”) — a warm, human-like, multi-language conversational AI that becomes the first intelligent layer of MBB’s operational structure. It qualifies leads, nurtures them, re-engages silent ones with strategic relance, tracks every interaction, feeds MAPS in real time, and frees the Hub team to focus on high-value human work — all while feeling exactly like a helpful young Congolese team member.

Strategic Objectives (Aligned with the Four Pillars + MAPS)

1. Operational Excellence (Pilier 2)
  * Achieve <60-second first response for 100 % of incoming WhatsApp messages.
  * Handle 80–85 % of all messages end-to-end without human intervention.
  * Guarantee resilience during blackouts and unstable networks (offline queuing + graceful resume).
2. Customer Experience & Engagement (Pilier 4)
  * Deliver natural, emoji-friendly, culturally warm conversations in Lingala/French/Swahili (auto-detect + seamless hybrid).
  * Make every interaction feel personal, respectful, and non-pushy (“help first, sell second”).
  * Implement a maximum-3-touch, value-first relance engine that respects RDC realities (blackout timing, city-specific tips, easy opt-out with “stop” / “arrête”).
3. Lead Conversion & Loyalty (Pilier 1 + Pilier 3)
  * Increase lead-to-order conversion via chatbot by minimum 30 % in Phase 1.
  * Automatically invite qualified leads to the Club and credit points.
  * Achieve 35–45 % response rate on 1st relance and <8 % opt-out rate overall.
4. MAPS Intelligence Loop (Pilier 1 – Core Engine)
  * Capture every micro-pattern from conversations (product requests, silence reasons, city/age preferences).
  * Automatically tag and feed insights to the Lab for next prototypes.
  * Turn relance outcomes into actionable data for timing, content, and product decisions.
5. Technical & Compliance Foundations
  * 100 % self-hosted, low-bandwidth-first, privacy-compliant (clear consent, data minimization).
  * Full integration with Hub CRM / Airtable for real-time Club data and order routing.
  * Simple conversion-funnel dashboard + CSV/Google Sheets export.
  * Modular architecture so additional channels (Telegram fallback, future web widget) can be added without rework.

Phased Success Metrics (Tied to Existing Roadmap)

* Phase 1 (0–6 months): Basic flows + 3 relance templates live on WhatsApp, trained on 500 real conversations, first-response <60 s, 80 % automation rate.
* Phase 2 (6–18 months): Full MAPS pattern recognition, voice-note support, dynamic relance.
* Phase 3: Predictive replies and advanced personalization.

Key Non-Functional Goals

* The bot must never feel robotic — weekly native tone audits + MAPS feedback loop.
* Must work during blackouts (queue + “Je suis toujours là même si le courant est coupé !”).
* Must respect cultural norms: short messages, light humor, local references, never sales pressure.
* Easy human escalation (<3 minutes) for voice notes, complex SAV, or high-value leads.

Expected Impact

* Higher lead-to-order conversion and Club growth.
* Faster product iteration via rich MAPS data.
* Reduced Hub-team workload → better focus on complex support and Zone events.
* Stronger competitive moat: MBB becomes the only ecosystem in RDC that turns WhatsApp chaos into structured intelligence while feeling deeply human and local.

Conclusion of Goal Statement

The Multi-Language Lead Nurturer Bot (MBB Assistant) is not an automation project — it is the concrete realization of “Structure as a Service.” By solving the exact pain points created by RDC realities, it will turn every incoming message and every silent lead into fuel for sustainable growth across all four pillars and the MAPS loop.
