

Multi-Language Lead Nurturer Bot Project

MBB – Structure as a Service Initiative

Date: April 2026

Prepared for: MBB Leadership & Toronto Supervision Team

Executive Summary

MBB is experiencing rapid growth in lead volume across Kinshasa, Lubumbashi, and Goma, yet the current manual WhatsApp Business + Hub-team process creates systemic bottlenecks that prevent the business from scaling efficiently while preserving the warm, personal, trust-based relationship Congolese customers expect. Leads go cold at an alarming rate, valuable MAPS patterns are lost, and operational capacity is wasted on repetitive tasks. The environmental, infrastructural, and cultural realities of the RDC amplify every weakness in the current system.

Current Reality – The Problem in Detail

1. High-Volume, Low-Resilience Lead Capture and Qualification Leads arrive primarily through WhatsApp (the only reliable channel), Facebook ads, Zone event QR codes, and Spot flyers. However, the Hub team cannot respond in <60 seconds for the majority of messages. Customers in Kinshasa often message during evenings or blackouts; if there is no instant reply, they move on or forget. Result: a large but invisible pool of “silent leads” that never enter the CRM.
2. Manual Message Management Creates Inconsistency and Burnout Every order inquiry, tracking request, SAV complaint, or Club question is handled manually. Tone varies by team member. Response times stretch beyond 24 hours during peak traffic or power cuts. Patterns that could feed the Lab (“80 % des pros à Gombe demandent des câbles 2 m renforcés”) are lost because no one has time to log them systematically.
3. Leads Go Cold Because of RDC Realities – Not Lack of Interest
  * Frequent power cuts and unstable 3G/4G cause conversations to drop mid-flow.
  * Customers are highly mobile (traffic in Gombe/Limete, moto-taxi culture) and price-sensitive due to inflation.
  * Many prefer voice notes or emoji-heavy Lingala-French-Swahili hybrid communication.
  * A simple delay of 24–48 hours feels like disinterest to the customer and leads to permanent silence. Current generic follow-ups (“Tu as vu mon message ?”) feel pushy and damage trust.
4. No Structured Relance / Reactivation Layer There is no systematic, value-first re-engagement process. Silent leads are either ignored or chased manually with the same noisy style, creating high opt-out risk and cultural friction. Maximum 3 relances are needed, but none exist today.
5. Lost MAPS Intelligence and Ecosystem Synergies Every conversation is a goldmine for the MAPS loop (Analyze → Ideate → Prototype → Scale). Today, 80–85 % of interactions are not captured in a structured way, starving the Lab of real-world durability feedback and the Club/Zone of loyalty insights.
6. Scalability Ceiling As MBB expands to new cities and product lines, the current manual model will collapse. Human escalation must be reserved for complex SAV and high-value cases only.

Environmental & Cultural Amplifiers (Why This Problem Is Unique to RDC)

* Infrastructure: Blackouts, dust, heat, flooding, narrow delivery windows.
* Digital Behavior: WhatsApp-native, Android-dominant, Mobile-Money-only payments.
* Cultural Expectation: Customers want to feel they are talking to a helpful young Congolese friend (“proximity”), not a robot or a distant company.
* Linguistic Reality: Auto-detection and seamless switching between Lingala (Kinshasa), French (everywhere), and Swahili (eastern cities) is mandatory.

Business Implications

* Lost revenue from cold leads (estimated 30–40 % of potential Box orders).
* Sub-optimal Club loyalty growth.
* Delayed product innovation because MAPS patterns are not harvested.
* Team burnout and inconsistent customer experience that risks damaging MBB’s hard-earned reputation for speed and personal touch.
* Competitive disadvantage: any new entrant with a better automated layer could steal market share in this WhatsApp-first market.

Conclusion of Problem Statement

The core problem is not lack of leads or lack of customer interest — it is the absence of a resilient, multilingual, culturally attuned, low-bandwidth-first automation layer that can handle 80–85 % of routine interactions instantly, re-engage silence strategically, capture MAPS intelligence automatically, and escalate only when truly necessary. Without this layer, MBB cannot achieve the “Structure as a Service” vision defined in the four pillars.
